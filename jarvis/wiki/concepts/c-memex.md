# Memex

## Definition

Vannevar Bush's 1945 vision of a personal, curated knowledge store in which the
*connections* between documents are as valuable as the documents themselves.

## How it works

A user builds associative trails linking related documents, creating a personal,
navigable web of knowledge — closer to a curated wiki than to what the web
actually became.

## Why it matters

It is the intellectual ancestor of the [[c-llm-wiki-pattern]]. The part Bush
couldn't solve — who maintains the trails and connections — is precisely what an
LLM now handles. ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]])
