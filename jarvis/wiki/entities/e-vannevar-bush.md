# Vannevar Bush

**Kind:** person

## What it is

Engineer who, in 1945, described the **Memex** — a personal, curated knowledge
store where the connections between documents are as valuable as the documents
themselves. Cited as the intellectual ancestor of the LLM Wiki.

## Key facts

- Proposed the [[c-memex]] in 1945. ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]])
- His vision's unsolved problem was *who does the maintenance* — the LLM Wiki
  answers it. ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]])

## Relationships

- Originator of [[c-memex]], a precursor to [[c-llm-wiki-pattern]]
