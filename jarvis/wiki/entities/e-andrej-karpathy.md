# Andrej Karpathy

**Kind:** person

## What it is

AI researcher who proposed the **LLM Wiki** pattern for building personal
knowledge bases with LLMs, where a large fraction of token throughput goes into
maintaining a structured wiki rather than manipulating code.

## Key facts

- Originated the [[c-llm-wiki-pattern]] in two X posts plus a gist. ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]])
- Argued the maintenance burden — not reading or thinking — is what kills human
  wikis, and that LLMs eliminate it. ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]])
- Coined the framing "Obsidian is the IDE. The LLM is the programmer. The wiki
  is the codebase." ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]])
