# Obsidian

**Kind:** product

## What it is

A free markdown-based notes app used as the "vault" for the LLM Wiki. Its graph
view visualizes the `[[wikilink]]` connections the LLM maintains, and its Web
Clipper drops clean markdown copies of web pages into `raw/`.

## Key facts

- Holds the wiki as plain markdown — portable, git-friendly, no lock-in. ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]])
- In Karpathy's framing, "Obsidian is the IDE." ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]])
- The Web Clipper is configured to save into the immutable `raw/` folder. ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]])
